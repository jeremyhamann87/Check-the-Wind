      Disclaimers & Data Sources

      Weather. Wind speed, gusts, direction, temperature, forecasts & weather alerts come live from the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), NOAA. Coverage is limited to U.S. locations & territories. Forecasts update hourly at the source; reload to refresh.

      Radar. Current radar is NOAA NEXRAD imagery served by Iowa State University's Iowa Environmental Mesonet. The expanded radar uses NOAA MRMS observations & NOAA HRRR model forecasts, which Check the Wind processes and extends a short way ahead using the recent motion of the storms. The forecast part is an estimate and storms can change quickly.

      Air quality & pollen. The Air Quality Index & Pollutants (PM2.5) are sourced live from AirNow (airnow.gov), EPA — coverage depends on the nearest reporting monitor. Hourly air-quality & recent-rainfall figures used for Best Times come from Open-Meteo. Pollen isn't available from any free public U.S. source, so trees/grasses/weeds levels are a seasonal estimate based on location & time of year, not a measurement.

      Places, maps & sun times. Place search uses Open-Meteo's geocoding service (GeoNames data, CC BY 4.0); place names for “Use my location” come from the National Weather Service. Map tiles are © OpenStreetMap contributors. Sunrise & sunset times by SUNRISE-SUNSET.ORG.

      Best Times. Best Times are a ranking calculated by Check the Wind from the forecast, air-quality and rainfall data above, using default limits that you can change with Customize. They are a guide, not a guarantee.

      Your information. Check the Wind has no accounts and no analytics or advertising trackers. Your favorites, recent places, Best Times settings, animation preference & last location are stored only in this browser, on your device; clearing your browser's site data removes them. To show you weather, the places you search for — and your approximate coordinates if you tap “Use my location” & allow it — are sent to the services listed above. This page loads its code library & map software from cdnjs and its font from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address as any website you visit can.

      Please note. Forecasts, alerts & Best Times are provided for general information, as-is & without warranty, and can be wrong or delayed. They are not a substitute for official warnings. In severe weather follow weather.gov & your local officials, and call 911 in an emergency.
